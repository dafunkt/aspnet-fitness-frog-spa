Purchase-to-Pay

PR-to-PO-to-Three-Way Matching

PR to PO to Three-Way Matching

This process ensures that purchases are properly approved, goods/services are receipted, and invoices are matched accurately before payment. It supports financial control, audit readiness, and accurate reporting.

PR-PO-Three-Way Matching Process Map

Step-by-Step Guide

Item/Service Details Entered
GL Code and Cost Centre Correct
Supporting Documents Attached
Submitted for Approval
Track Status in ARM
1
Raise a Purchase Requisition (PR)
  • Initiated in Epicor ARM by the requester
  • Includes item/service details, cost centre, and justification.
2
PR Approval Workflow
  • Approval trees are preset based on amount thresholds and cost centres
  • Approvers are notified via Epicor or email
3
Purchase Order (PO) Created
  • Once approved, Epicor generates a PO
  • PO is sent to the vendor for fulfilment
4
Goods/Services Receipt
  • Requester or receiving team confirms delivery in Epicor
  • Receipt entry is essential for matching and must be attached with supporting documents
5
Invoice Submission
  • Vendor send invoice to invoices@scrosswa.org.au
  • The invoice's information is extracted via KOFAX and sent to ARM
6
Three-Way Matching
  • Epicor ARM uses the PO number to compare the PO, Invoice and goods receipt
  • If matched, the invoice is automatically approved for payment.
  • If mismatched, the invoice is sent to Drafts for AP to investigate and resolve.

Purchase Requisitions (PR)

A Purchase Requisition (PR) is the first step in SCCWA's Procure-to-Pay (P2P) process. It is an internal request to purchase goods or services and ensures that all purchases are properly authorised and documented before a Purchase Order is raised.

This is when a Capital Expenditure cost needs to be identified. For more information regarding Cap Ex please go to: (Link to page)

Purpose of PRs

1
Provide financial control and auditability
2
Ensure correct GL coding and cost allocation
3
Enable three-way matching downstream
