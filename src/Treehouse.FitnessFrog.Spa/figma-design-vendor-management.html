Vendor Management

Vendor Onboarding

Best purchasing practice in SCCWA's purchase to pay framework involves onboarding a vendor before any purchase is initiated. Most of the goods and services that SCCWA requires for operations and administration can be purchased from established vendors.

Staff are expected to use the Check Vendor Status tool on the procurement site before making any purchase. If they wish to use a vendor not setup and active in the system they will submit a Sourcing Ticket with procurement. The procurement team will then initiate the sourcing process and complete the Vendor Setup once a contract is agreed upon.

Vendor Not Setup Before Invoice Received

This process is if an invoice is received before a vendor is setup in the system.

Invoice Received before vendor setup

An invoice received without a vendor setup will be flagged for verification in KOFAX or sent to Drafts in Epicor.

An AP officer will review the invoice error and download a PDF version of the invoice.

Raise a Procurement Ticket

Go to the Procurement Helpdesk (Support → Procurement Helpdesk) and select 'Setup a New Vendor'.

Once the form is loaded fill in the relevant details, upload the invoice copy and submit the form.

An AP officer will review the invoice error and download a PDF version of the invoice.

Procurement Complete Vendor Setup

A procurement officer will set the vendor up in KOFAX and Epicor.

The AP officer who raised the ticket will be notified once the setup is complete.

Process Invoice

Process the invoice as normal.

Vendor Detail Changes

This is the process for when a Vendor's information, such as account details, change after they have been put into the system.

Vendor Communications

To ensure consistency, compliance, and timely processing of invoices, all vendor communications must follow the guidelines below:

11
Invoice Submission

All vendors must send their invoices directly to:
invoices@scrosswa.org.au

Invoices should not be forwards by staff or submitted via personal email accounts.

Important:

  • Invoices must be attached as PDF files to the email.
  • Do not forward emails or attach emails within emails — KOFAX can only process the invoices that are attached as standalone documents.

Business Support Officers (BSOs) Must redirect any suppliers attempting to send invoice to them. Vendors should always submit invoice directly to the AP Inbox.

22
Payment & General Enquiries

For any queries related to payments, invoice status, or account details, vendors should contact:
accpay@scrosswa.org.au

This centralised inbox ensures that all communications are tracked and responded to efficiently by the AP team.

33
Internal Communications Protocol
  • AP Officers must use standardised response templates for common queries.
    — See Onboarding Resources [link].
  • All supplier communications must be sent from the accpay@scrosswa.org.au address.
  • Do not use personal work emails when contacting vendors — this helps maintain a professional and auditable communication trail.
