Step-by-Step Guide

1

Raise a Purchase Requisition (PR)

  • Initiated in Epicor ARM by the requester
  • Includes item/service details, cost centre, and justification.
2

PR Approval Workflow

  • Approval trees are preset based on amount thresholds and cost centre codes
  • Approvers are notified via Epicor or email
3

Purchase Order (PO) Created

  • Once approved, Epicor generates a PO
  • PO is sent to the vendor for fulfilment
4

Goods/Services Receipt

  • Requester or receiving team confirms delivery in Epicor
  • Receipt entry is essential for matching and must be attached when receipting the PO
5

Invoice Submission

  • Vendor send invoice to invoices@scrosswa.org.au
  • The invoice's information is extracted via KOFAX and sent to ARM
6

Three-Way Matching

  • Epicor ARM uses the PO number to compare the PO, Invoice and goods receipt
  • If matched, the invoice is automatically approved for payment.
  • If mismatched, the invoice is sent to Drafts for AP to investigate and resolve.
Purchase Requisitions (PR)

A Purchase Requisition (PR) is the first step in SCCWA's Procure-to-Pay (P2P) process. It is an internal request to purchase goods or services and ensures that all purchases are properly authorized, budgeted, and compliant with SCCWA's financial controls. Once approved, the PR automatically generates a Purchase Order (PO) in Epicor ARM.

This is when a Capital Expenditure cost needs to be identified. For more information regarding Cap Ex please go to: (Link to page)

Purpose of PRs

1
Provide financial control and auditability
2
Ensure purchases align with budgets and Delegation of Authority
3
Form the basis for PO creation and subsequent invoice matching

AP Considerations

A PO Number must be present on the PO and Invoice.
Details have to be consistent between the PR, PO, GRN/DD and invoice.
Goods Receipt Note (GRN) or Delivery Docket (DD) must be attached in Epicor for Three-Way Matching.
The PO creation triggers Epicor's WIP Staging, please see [Link to page] to learn more.

Purchase Order

Purchase Orders (POs) are the backbone of the procurement and payment process in Epicor Advanced Requisition Management (ARM). They ensure every purchase is authorized, tracked, and matched to receipts and invoices, supporting compliance and efficient operations.

What is a Purchase Order?

Definition

A PO is a formal document generated by ARM after a requisition is approved. It authorizes the purchase of goods or services from a supplier.

Purpose

1
Legal proof of purchase
2
Reference for matching invoices and receipts
3
Ensures budget control and auditability

AP considerations

Ensure every invoice references a valid PO number
All POs must be receipted in ARM before the invoice can be match.
For three-way-matching the invoice needs to have all line items recorded (do not roll up)
ARM allows for partial matches of invoices to POs
All supporting documents should be attached to the PO and receipt records
Investigate and resolve any discrepancies before payment
Hold invoices for cancelled POs and document exceptions
