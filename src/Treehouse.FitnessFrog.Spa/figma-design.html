What Finance Needs to Know

How Purchasing Works at SCCWA?

Purchasing is the first step in the Procure-to-Pay (P2P) process and directly impacts how invoices are processed, matched, and paid. While Procurement manages sourcing and vendor setup, Finance and AP ensure purchases are correctly coded, approved, receipted, and reconciled.

This guide outlines what AP needs to know to support purchasing activities effectively.

Finance & AP Responsibilities

Vendor Setup

Confirm vendor is active in Epicor/KOFAX. If not, raise a ticket via the Procurement Helpdesk.

PR Raised

Ensure PR includes correct GL code, cost centre, and justification.
Validate CapEx if applicable.

PR Approved

Approvals follow SCCWA's Delegation of Authority (DoA) Matrix. AP may need to verify approval paths.

PO Created

PO must match PR details and vendor info. PO number must appear on the invoice.

Goods/Services Received

Receipting must be completed in Epicor. Missing receipts delay matching and payment.

Invoice Received

Invoices must be sent to invoices@scrosswa.org.au as PDFs. AP validates invoice details against PO and receipt.

Three-Way Matching

Epicor ARM matches PO, invoice, and receipt. Mismatches are flagged for AP to investigate.

Payment Processed

AP confirms payment method (e.g., NAB Connect, Corporate Card) and ensures audit trail is complete.

Common Scenarios & AP Actions

Invoice Received before vendor setup

Raise a vendor setup ticket via Procurement Helpdesk and hold invoice until setup is complete.

Missing PO or GRN

Flag the mismatch, obtain approval for variance, and update records accordingly.

Duplicate invoice detected
